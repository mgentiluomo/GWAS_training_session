# Figure prompts for the lecture slides

Written for the course author. Each slide that has a figure shows a visible placeholder on the page. Use the prompt below in Gemini, check the picture, and add it to the site yourself. I did not generate, copy or take any picture. The pictures must show no logo, no brand name and no real data. The slides are on `/teaching/gwas/lecture-slides`.

Every prompt ends with the same rule: "No logos, no brand names, no real data and no text inside the picture other than short labels."

## Allele counts in cases and controls

- Placeholder id: `slide-association`
- Alt text draft: Two bars side by side show the share of allele G in cases and in controls. The bar for cases is taller.
- Prompt for Gemini: Simple flat infographic for students: two stacked bars, one for cases and one for controls, each split into two colours for two alleles. The cases bar has a larger share of the first allele. Calm colours, white background. No logos, no brand names, no real data and no text inside the picture other than short labels.

## Allele frequency and effect size

- Placeholder id: `slide-architecture`
- Alt text draft: A plot with allele frequency on the horizontal axis and effect size on the vertical axis. A diagonal band shows where GWAS can detect variants.
- Prompt for Gemini: Schematic plot for students. Horizontal axis: allele frequency from very rare to common, on a log scale. Vertical axis: effect size on risk from small to large. Mark three zones with labels: common variants with small effect (found by GWAS), rare variants with large effect (found by sequencing and family studies), and an unlikely zone at the top right. Flat design, calm colours. No logos, no brand names, no real data and no text inside the picture other than short labels.
