**Correction to the feasibility report of 23 November.** The first version of `panel_count.py` matched
variants by ID. The IDs of `all_hg38_rs_noannot.pvar.zst` are rsIDs or `.`, so different variants shared a
key. The panel counts in the report were right (they did not use IDs), but the share of donor variants that
are also in the panel was not. Matched by position, REF and ALT, the right numbers are:

| Region | Polymorphic in the 212 EUR donors | Also polymorphic in the panel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| chr18, L4 plus 1 Mb each side (36,792,715 to 39,292,715) | 14,968 | 13,900 (93%) |
| chr22, 35.0 to 37.5 Mb | 20,199 | 18,974 (94%) |
| chr22, 36.0 to 36.5 Mb | 4,523 | 4,276 (95%) |

The script in the repository uses the corrected matching.
